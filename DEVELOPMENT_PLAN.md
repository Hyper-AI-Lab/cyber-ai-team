# Cyber-Team Production Closure Ledger

## Product Contract

Cyber-Team is an autonomous, evidence-driven company operating system. Company evidence
drives its living business model, domains, roles, mandates, objectives, workflows,
capabilities, and integrations. The Chief Operating Agent coordinates work, the Observer
independently challenges it, and the owner can inspect, instruct, pause, take over, or
approve material actions.

ERPNext is the canonical business system of record. PostgreSQL stores control state and
durable history, Qdrant supports semantic memory, Temporal provides durable execution,
OPA and OpenFGA enforce action and relationship authorization, and the Owner Console is
the human control plane.

## Accepted Foundation

- Dynamic company intelligence, operating-model synthesis, domain lifecycle, roles,
  mandates, business events, work items, workflows, outcomes, and reflections.
- Chief Operating Agent, independent Observer, operation graph, role/capability growth,
  outsourcing requests, and owner takeover controls.
- Live ERPNext and SMTP/IMAP integrations with optional communication providers disabled
  until evidence makes them necessary.
- Approval-bound side effects, permanent action gates, prompt-injection quarantine,
  audit/control evidence, backup/restore automation, and immutable release deployment.
- Release `0.4.14` passed the strict 24-hour staging soak with `289/289` samples.

## Completion Definition

The staging release candidate is complete only when:

- unchanged evidence produces no duplicate claim or operating-model revision;
- all active domains and agents have evidence-backed objectives, mandates, authority,
  KPIs, and health;
- every gap, request, approval, signal, and work item has a truthful disposition;
- no autonomous side effect can bypass OPA, OpenFGA, Observer, or approval policy;
- audit and lifecycle persistence remain bounded under the 24-hour soak;
- privacy, observability, restore, load, workflow, security, and browser gates pass;
- public push, manual, and scheduled GitHub CI are green for the released SHA.

Production promotion remains a separate permanent owner decision.
